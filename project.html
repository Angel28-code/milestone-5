I plan to make a website that gathers data from the NCAA website to help people find out more about the statistics of a team winning and takedown chances.

Top 10 teams by year

Each point shows a team’s championship points for that year, with the school logo and team name. Hover over a point for the coach and exact values. Rankings and points are from the NCAA Division I wrestling records; logos are loaded from official athletics sites and their image CDNs.
